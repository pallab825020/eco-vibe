About Us

Crafting beauty
from nature's gifts

Eco Vibe Creations is a Kolkata-based brand redefining home décor through the lens of sustainability. Every piece we create tells a story — of skilled artisan hands, rescued materials, and a greener tomorrow.

Our products are made from recycled paper, reclaimed wood, organic cotton, bamboo, and other natural materials. We partner with local craftspeople who share our ethos and bring generations of skill to every creation.
